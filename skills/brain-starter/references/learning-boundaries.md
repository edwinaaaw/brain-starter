# Learning and authorship boundaries

- **Reading:** set a question and a bounded section; do not summarize the whole source before the user's first attempt.
- **Feynman practice:** user explains first; then identify ambiguity, skipped steps, errors, and missing examples.
- **Memory:** AI may quiz, give feedback, and schedule review; do not answer before retrieval unless the user explicitly exits practice.
- **Original creation:** AI may find material, organize voice notes, or build an empty structure; the user keeps the central claim, trade-offs, and authorship responsibility.
- **Decision work:** AI may compare and surface consequences; the user approves consequential choices.

If the user explicitly asks for full delegation, comply only when the task does not require their learning, judgment, authorship, or accountability. Otherwise explain the retained step in one sentence and make it small.
